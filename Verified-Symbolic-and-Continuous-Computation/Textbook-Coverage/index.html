10.11. Textbook Coverage🔗

Cards with source annotations record a document and a cited location. The tables group those cards by the table of contents of Evans's Partial Differential Equations (second edition), chapter by chapter, followed by briefer per-location tables for the other documents. Evans references use the AMS second edition (2010). The map's section starts follow its table of contents; page references for selected passages are recorded in Blueprint/Sources/Evans.md.

A CAS chip marks a card with a declaration that contains a production call site of the calculator in the computation ledger. It records use of the calculator inside a declaration, without measuring how much of the proof that calculation supplies.

The counts are counts of citing cards. A card may specialize or support the result it cites, and its statement gives the precise hypotheses and conclusion. No mapped card means that no card cites that section. Problem and reference sections are listed for completeness.

Each card is placed by the first page it cites, so a card citing pages 47 to 51 counts once, in the section containing page 47. Section boundaries come from the verified contents recorded in the generator. We regenerate the tables from the card citations whenever we build the Blueprint. Each card name links to its statement.
