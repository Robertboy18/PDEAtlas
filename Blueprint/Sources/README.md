# Blueprint source records

These records identify the mathematical sources cited by Blueprint statement
cards: authors, editions or versions, and publisher or institutional links.
Each record names the edition that its page and section locators refer to.

In a structured source annotation, the text span points to lines of one of
these records, not to an excerpt of the cited work, and the page field gives the
locator in the cited work: a printed page, a chapter, or an equation number.
A citation locates the mathematical background of a card; the Lean statement on
the card gives the hypotheses and conclusion that are proved.
