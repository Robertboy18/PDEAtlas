# Vorticity and Incompressible Flow

Andrew J. Majda and Andrea L. Bertozzi, *Vorticity and Incompressible Flow*,
Cambridge Texts in Applied Mathematics, Cambridge University Press, 2001.

Official source:
https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9780511613203

LeanPDE cites Chapter 1 for the continuum equations, material derivative,
incompressibility, and the Euler and Navier–Stokes equations. The vorticity
and Bernoulli cards cite Chapters 1–2.

Cambridge's current record dates print publication to 26 November 2001 and
digital publication to 3 February 2010. The bibliography uses the print date.
